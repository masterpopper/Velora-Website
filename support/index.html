CONTACT SUPPORT

Tell us what’s happening.

Report a problem or share an idea from your phone or computer. No need to type a report on your TV.

You’re in the right place: veloratv.app/support

Your feedback

Start with a title and a description. You’ll review your report on GitHub before submitting it.

What happened, and what did you expect? For a problem, include your device, Velora version and steps to reproduce it.

GitHub sign-in is required. Submitted reports are public and linked to your GitHub account. Don’t include passwords, private stream links, email addresses or other personal information.

Opens a prefilled issue in a new tab. Nothing is posted until you submit it on GitHub.

Before you send

A specific example helps us understand the problem. Mention the section you were using, the button you pressed, and any error message.
